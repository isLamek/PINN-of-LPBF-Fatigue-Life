Phase 1: finding the defects

A neural network marks the defects in every CT slice, with a mean Dice of 0.805 against a criterion of 0.80.

Governing equation

A network $f_\theta$ maps a CT slice $x$ to a defect probability per pixel, fitted by empirical risk minimisation, $\theta^{*}=\arg\min_\theta \tfrac{1}{N}\sum_i \mathcal{L}\big(f_\theta(x_i),y_i\big)$, with $\mathcal{L}$ the sum of Dice and cross-entropy losses. Agreement is scored by $\mathrm{Dice}=2|P\cap G|/(|P|+|G|)$.

Numerical scheme

nnU-Net v2, two-dimensional, trained 200 epochs on 16-bit slices (patch 768 × 768, batch 1). Every defect in the resulting mask is isolated and sized as √area.

Verification

Five-fold cross-validation (Dice 0.794 to 0.825 per fold), and the governing defect size on 14 cylinders never seen in training against the reference labels: R² = 0.986.

Result

Mean Dice 0.805: the 0.80 criterion of H1 is met.

Dice measures the overlap between the predicted defect pixels and the reference labels, from 0 to 1. Defects fill a tiny fraction of each slice, which makes Dice a harsh score, so 0.80 already means close agreement.

Why precision above recall is the safer error

Precision (0.862) is above recall (0.841): the network misses slightly more true defect pixels than it invents. The next phase only needs the largest defect in the gauge. Trimming the edge of a large defect barely changes its √area, while inventing a large one that is not there would change the predicted life completely.

How the data were split

The 64 cylinders were split before any training: 46 training, 14 held out, and 4 not fatigue tested. The split is stratified by process regime (keyhole, spatter, clean) and by stress level (300 and 450 MPa), so neither set is dominated by one condition. The held-out cylinders are used throughout Phases 2 to 4, and no model was ever fitted on them.

Labels. The reference labels come from the originating team's own Laplacian-of-Gaussian, threshold and clustering pipeline, not from hand annotation, so the network is learning to reproduce a published method on unseen cylinders.

nnU-Net v2, 2-D, single-channel 16-bit input, two classes; RTX 3050 4 GB, patch 768 × 768, batch 1, 200 epochs, 5-fold cross-validation.
